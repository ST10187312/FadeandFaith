About Fade and Faith

Fade and Fade is a digital barber shop appointment and customer management application designed to make getting a professional fade haircut easier, faster, and more convenient.

Customers can create a profile, choose from available barbers, view available time slots, book appointments, manage their bookings, and earn credit points through their activity.

The application also helps the barber shop manage multiple barbers and their schedules in one organised system.

The Problem

Traditional barber shops often rely on walk-ins and manual scheduling, which can lead to:

* Long waiting times
* Uncertainty about barber availability
* Appointment conflicts
* Poor schedule management
* Difficulty maintaining customer loyalty

Fade and Fade addresses these problems by bringing the booking process into one simple digital platform.

How It Works

The customer journey is simple:

Create Profile → Choose Barber → Select Time → Book Fade → Visit Shop → Earn Credit Points

Customers can:

* Create and manage their profile
* View available barbers
* Select a preferred barber
* Choose an available appointment
* Manage their bookings
* Build an appointment history
* Earn credit points

Barbers and the business can manage schedules and appointments, helping reduce conflicts and improve daily organisation.

Key Features

Customer Profiles

Customers can create profiles containing their information, appointment history, and credit points.

 Multiple Barbers

The system supports multiple barbers, each with their own availability and schedule.

Appointment Booking

Customers can book available time slots instead of relying entirely on walk-ins.

Credit Points

Customers can earn points through their activity, creating the foundation for a loyalty and rewards system.

Appointment Management

Bookings are organised in one place, making it easier to manage upcoming appointments and schedules.
